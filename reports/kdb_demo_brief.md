## Executive view

KDB remains a very strong sovereign-linked policy bank. The commercial angle is more interesting than the headline credit story: it is a recurring international issuer, uses wholesale funding extensively, maintains large multi-currency programmes, and can generate DCM, bilateral funding, FX/CCS, syndication and project-finance opportunities.

The next meeting should focus less on proving KDB's strength and more on discovering what changed in its 2026 funding plan, overseas pipeline, preferred currencies/tenors and partner-bank selection criteria.

## Opportunity Intelligence

### 1. 2026 FX funding / DCM — High

**Public fact:** USD remains the dominant offshore funding currency, while 2025 funding was essentially completed by Dec 2025.  
**What we want to learn:** What changed in 2026 size, tenor, currency and timing.  
**Potential angle:** DCM, bilateral funding, money market, FX / cross-currency swaps.

### 2. Relationship-bank / bilateral funding — High

**Public fact:** KDB explicitly describes relationship-based bank loans as a backstop source.  
**What we want to learn:** When KDB values committed balance sheet versus capital-markets execution.  
**Potential angle:** Bilateral lines, liquidity support, relationship expansion.

### 3. ASEAN project / syndicated finance — Medium

**Public fact:** Manufacturing is 45.3% of operating exposure.  
**What we want to learn:** Which Korean corporate outbound projects are most active in ASEAN and where KDB wants international-bank participation.  
**Potential angle:** Syndication, PF, guarantees, risk participation, transaction banking.

## Meeting Discovery Playbook

1. **Funding plan:** “What has changed in KDB's 2026 offshore funding plan versus 2025 — size, tenor or currency mix?”
2. **Currency economics:** “At current USD levels, are EUR, GBP, AUD or other currencies becoming more attractive after hedging?”
3. **Partner-bank role:** “Where is international-bank participation most useful in KDB-led financings: balance sheet, distribution, local network or execution?”
4. **ASEAN pipeline:** “Which ASEAN countries or sectors are taking the most attention from Korean corporate clients this year?”
5. **Wallet allocation:** “When allocating wallet to external banks, what has become more important versus last year?”
