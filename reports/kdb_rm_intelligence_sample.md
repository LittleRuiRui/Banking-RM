# KDB RM Intelligence Brief — Show Sample
**Client:** Korea Development Bank (KDB)  
**As of:** 26 Aug 2026  
**Purpose:** Institutional / FI RM meeting preparation prototype

## Executive view

**Credit:** Strong, with sovereign linkage central to the credit story. KDB is 100% owned by the Korean government and its international ratings are aligned closely with Korea's sovereign standing.

**Commercial angle:** KDB is not just a credit counterparty. Its recurring international funding, policy-finance mandate and cross-border project activity create potential DCM, bilateral funding, bond-investment, FX/CCS, syndication and project-finance opportunities.

**Best meeting objective:** Do not spend the meeting proving that KDB is strong. Use public facts to uncover what has changed in its funding mix, overseas priorities, partner-bank selection criteria and upcoming pipeline.

## 1. Credit Intelligence

### What matters most
1. **Sovereign linkage / government support** — monitor sovereign rating and statutory support.
2. **Capital** — watch whether policy-driven balance-sheet growth materially outpaces internal capital generation.
3. **Asset quality** — focus on policy-sensitive sectors and restructuring exposures.
4. **Funding** — international wholesale funding is both a liquidity topic and a commercial opportunity.

## 2. Opportunity Intelligence

### A. USD / multi-currency funding — HIGH
KDB maintains:
- Global MTN programme: **US$30bn**
- USCP programme: **US$5bn**
- ECP programme: **US$12bn**

Potential angles: DCM participation, bond investment, bilateral funding, money-market placement, FX / cross-currency swaps.

### B. Overseas project / syndicated finance — HIGH
Potential angles: project finance, syndication, risk participation and guarantees for Korean corporate expansion in ASEAN / US.

## 3. Meeting Discovery Playbook

| What you want to know | Better indirect question | Follow-up | Product angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2027 FX funding pipeline | “How are you thinking about the mix between benchmark issuance and more opportunistic private placements going into next year?” | “Has USD versus EUR or other currencies changed for you?” | DCM, PP, FX/CCS |
| Effect of long USD yields | “Has the current long-end USD rate environment changed how you think about tenor or timing?” | “Are shorter-dated or alternative-currency markets becoming more relevant?” | Funding, CCS, MM |
| ASEAN pipeline | “Are you seeing more Korean corporate financing activity in the US, or is Southeast Asia becoming more active?” | “Which countries or sectors are most active?” | PF, syndication |
| Buying criteria | “For recent offshore transactions, what has mattered most when selecting banking partners — pricing, balance sheet, distribution, or regional network?” | “Has that changed from last year?” | Pitch positioning |
| Currency diversification | “My impression is that USD remains the core offshore market, but diversification has become more important. Is that still fair?” | “Which currencies are realistic alternatives?” | FX, CCS, issuance |
| What changed | “What has moved up KDB's priority list this year compared with last year?” | “What is driving that change?” | Pipeline discovery |

## 4. Conversation technique

**Ask about change, not static strategy.**  
Instead of “What is your funding strategy?”, ask: “What has changed in how you think about funding mix versus last year?”

**Offer a hypothesis the client can correct.**  
“My impression is that USD remains core, but diversification is becoming more important. Is that still the case?”

**Move from market → client → implication.**  
“Long-end USD yields have stayed elevated.” → “Has that changed how you think about tenor?” → “Where would you want more support from banking partners?”

## 5. Early-warning / business triggers

**RED:** Korea sovereign downgrade, material change to government support, sharp capital deterioration, major asset-quality shock.

**AMBER:** material NPL / restructuring increase, unusual funding-spread widening, sharp change in FX funding dependence, major policy-mandate change.

**GREEN:** new benchmark bond, overseas financing programme, Korean corporate outbound project, syndication/PF mandate, funding-currency shift.

## 6. Suggested RM next action

Leave the next KDB meeting with answers to four questions:
1. What is changing in KDB's 12–18 month offshore funding plan?
2. Which overseas sectors / geographies generate the most financing activity?
3. Where does KDB want more international-bank participation?
4. What does KDB value most when allocating wallet to banking partners?
